# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release. An unofficial X / Twitter client for iOS 6 (universal armv7, and an arm64 build for iOS 14–17), in
English and Czech, in the classic iOS 6 blue-bird style. Five tabs: Home, Explore, Notifications, Messages, Me.

- Home timeline with a For You / Following switch, pull-to-refresh and infinite scroll.
- Tweet threads: the reply chain above a tweet, the focal tweet (bigger, with tappable links), and the replies.
- Profiles: banner, avatar, bio, counts (tappable → followers / following), a Tweets / Replies / Media / Likes
  switch, and Follow / Message buttons.
- Search: Top, Latest and People; current trends and your recent searches when nothing is being searched.
- Media: photos (pinch to zoom) and videos / GIFs, played through the app's own TLS proxy.
- **Actions**: reply, repost (and undo), like, bookmark and the … menu (quote, copy link, share, open in browser,
  delete your own) — right on each tweet.
- **Compose**: new tweets, replies and quotes, with an optional photo from the library.
- **Follow / unfollow** from a profile.
- **Notifications** (All / Mentions) and **Direct Messages** (conversations and sending).
- Retweets, quote tweets, reply context, verified badges, counts and view counts; dark and light themes; the
  sister-app link chooser (Tubie / Twitcher / Kicker / TikTak / Surfari).
- A helper (`pi/`) that signs in with your own X account through `twikit` and does the reading and the actions.

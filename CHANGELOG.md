# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release. An unofficial read-only X / Twitter client for iOS 6 (universal armv7, and an arm64 build for
iOS 14–17), in English and Czech, in the classic iOS 6 blue-bird style.

- Home timeline with a For You / Following switch, pull-to-refresh and infinite scroll.
- Tweet threads: the reply chain above a tweet, the focal tweet (bigger, with tappable links), and the replies.
- Profiles: banner, avatar, bio, counts, and a Tweets / Replies / Media / Likes switch.
- Search: Top, Latest and People; current trends and your recent searches when nothing is being searched.
- Media: photos (pinch to zoom) and videos / GIFs, played through the app's own TLS proxy.
- Retweets, quote tweets, reply context, verified badges, counts and view counts.
- Bookmarks kept on the device; dark and light themes; the sister-app link chooser (Tubie / Twitcher / Kicker /
  TikTak / Surfari).
- A helper (`pi/`) that signs in with your own X account through `twikit` and hands the app small JSON.
